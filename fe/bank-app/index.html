Move Money

Send twice fires the same idempotency key twice. The money must move once — that is what makes a timed-out request safe to reissue.
Two windows on one account, both withdrawing: the ledger serializes them through the Raft log, so exactly the available funds can be withdrawn and the balance never goes negative. The loser gets insufficient funds, not a corrupted balance.
